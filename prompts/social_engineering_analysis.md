# Use Case: Behavioral Phishing Analysis
This prompt uses Gemini CLI to decode psychological triggers in phishing messages, specifically focusing on social engineering and bypass tactics.

## Prompt:
"Analyze the attached alert.txt. Identify psychological triggers like 'urgency' and 'fear'. Explain how it manipulates user behavior to bypass critical thinking."

## Input Example (alert.txt):
"Your account will be suspended in 2 hours. Click here to verify immediately!"
